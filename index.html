Frequently asked questions

Is TuitionPro really free?

Yes. All features are free to use.

Where is my data stored?

In this version, data is stored securely in your own browser. A cloud database (Supabase) can be connected later so your data syncs across devices.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes, the design adapts to phones, tablets and computers.
